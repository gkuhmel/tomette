## App Store · Description (identique sur Google Play) (1383/4000)

```text
Tomettes is an action puzzle inspired by the hexagonal terracotta tiles of Provençal homes.

Swap tiles diagonally and match three colours or more… while the stack keeps rising! Tiles fall, new matches form and the chains keep coming: ×2, ×3, ×7…

EASY TO PICK UP
• One gesture: slide a tile towards its diagonal neighbour
• A tutorial played right on the board
• 4 difficulty levels, from Relaxed to Extreme

IDEAS TO DISCOVER
• Rosettes: surround a tile with one colour to earn the golden tile, which clears a whole colour
• Orders: make the requested pattern for a big bonus
• Cracked tiles, sealed tiles, jokers and mortar slabs to spice things up

7 WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: how long can you last?
• Time attack: 2 minutes for your best score
• Mosaic: as many rosettes as you can in 3 minutes
• Express: start at level 8, things move fast
• Campaign: 30 stages with a finish line, up to 3 stars each
• Expedition: 20 stages, a single life and a card to pick after each stage, with its perk and its catch
• Duel: take on the computer, or challenge a friend online with a simple code

AND ALSO
• Daily missions and a player level
• 44 achievements, statistics, themes and icon sets to unlock
• Plays offline (except the online duel)
• No ads, no account: your games stay on your phone

The terracotta tiles found in every home in the South of France have never been this much fun.
```
